# Video Tracker

Video Tracker is a Moodle activity that lets teachers add video-based learning
to a course while giving learners a reliable way to resume playback and see
their progress.

Teachers can use a video uploaded to Moodle, a YouTube video, a public or
unlisted Vimeo video, or a direct HTML5 video URL. The activity records each
learner's watched percentage and last position, sends the resulting percentage
to the Moodle gradebook, and can complete the activity when a configured
viewing threshold is reached.

## Key features

- Moodle-hosted video uploads
- YouTube and Vimeo embeds
- Direct HTML5 video URLs
- Optional poster image and configurable player ratio
- Per-learner watched percentage and resume position
- Percentage-based activity completion
- Moodle gradebook integration
- Teacher engagement report with status, search, and group filters
- Individual and filtered bulk progress reset
- Administrator gradebook diagnostics and recovery links
- Moodle backup and restore
- Moodle Privacy API support
- Moodle App integration

## Common use cases

Video Tracker can be used for training videos, recorded lessons, demonstrations,
onboarding material, compliance content, flipped-classroom preparation, and
other activities where teachers need visibility into learner viewing progress.

## Privacy and external video providers

Uploaded videos and tracking data remain in Moodle. If a teacher selects
YouTube, Vimeo, or another external URL, the learner's browser connects to that
provider. Site administrators should include applicable external providers in
their privacy documentation.

Video Tracker Free does not require a licence key, trial registration, external
account, or paid LearnPlug service.
