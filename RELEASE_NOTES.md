# Video Tracker 1.2.1 Starter

Build: `2026091800`

Video Tracker 1.2.1 Starter is the US$19 edition distributed through Moodle
Marketplace.

## Highlights

- Supports Moodle-hosted, YouTube, Vimeo, and direct HTML5 videos.
- Tracks viewing percentage and resume position.
- Integrates with Moodle grades and activity completion.
- Provides teacher engagement reports and progress reset tools.
- Adds an administrator gradebook recovery screen for broken calculations.
- Includes Moodle App, Privacy API, backup, and restore integration.
- Completes the Starter edition rebranding across package documentation and
  metadata.
- Improves the accessibility and reliability of the localized completion-goal
  tooltip without relying on JavaScript.
- Includes the latest gradebook diagnostic database-performance improvements.
- Uses Moodle's specialized DML methods for filtered progress resets.

## Edition scope

This release requires no runtime licence checks, trial workflow, customer
registration, or external account. CSV export, subtitle workflows, learning
objectives, playback policies, commercial updates, and priority customer
support remain part of Video Tracker Pro.

## Compatibility

- Moodle 4.5 through 5.2
- Stable maturity
