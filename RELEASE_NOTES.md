# Video Tracker 1.2

Build: `2026072402`

Video Tracker 1.2 is the updated Free edition for the Moodle Plugins directory.

## Highlights

- Supports Moodle-hosted, YouTube, Vimeo, and direct HTML5 videos.
- Tracks viewing percentage and resume position.
- Integrates with Moodle grades and activity completion.
- Provides teacher engagement reports and progress reset tools.
- Adds an administrator gradebook recovery screen for broken calculations.
- Includes Moodle App, Privacy API, backup, and restore integration.

## Edition scope

This release contains no licence checks, trial workflow, customer registration,
or CSV export. CSV export, additional analytics, subtitle workflows, learning
objectives, playback policies, commercial updates, and customer support remain
part of Video Tracker Pro.

## Compatibility

- Moodle 4.5 through 5.2
- Stable maturity
